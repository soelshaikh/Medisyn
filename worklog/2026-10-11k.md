## Bug Fixed

| Error | Cause | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| TS2345 `string | string[]` not assignable to `string` (8 occurrences) | `@types/express@5` types `req.params` values as `string | string[]`; `req.params.id!` only removes null, not `string[]` | Replaced all `req.params.id!` with `(req.params['id'] as string)` |
